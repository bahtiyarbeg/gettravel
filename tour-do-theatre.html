About This Experience

DO Vietnamese Theatre is Nha Trang's premier cultural entertainment venue showcasing authentic Vietnamese performing arts! Experience an enchanting evening of traditional music, elegant dance performances, folk storytelling, and stunning cultural costumes in a modern state-of-the-art theatre.

This spectacular 90-minute show combines ancient Vietnamese traditions with contemporary stage production, featuring talented artists performing classical instruments, regional folk dances, and dramatic storytelling that brings Vietnamese history and legends to life!

Perfect for culture enthusiasts, families, and travelers seeking authentic Vietnamese heritage experiences!

Performance Highlights

Traditional Instruments

Dan Bau (monochord), Dan Tranh (zither), bamboo flute performances

Folk Dances

Ethnic minority dances, fan dance, conical hat dance, royal court dance

Dragon & Lion Dance

Spectacular acrobatic performances with traditional costumes

Vietnamese Legends

Storytelling of ancient myths, historical tales, cultural heritage
