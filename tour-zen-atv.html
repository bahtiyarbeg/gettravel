Half Day

About This Experience

Get your adrenaline pumping at Zen Valley ATV Park – Nha Trang's most thrilling off-road quad biking adventure! Ride powerful 250cc ATVs through challenging terrain including mountain trails, jungle paths, muddy river crossings, rice paddy fields, and steep hill climbs. Perfect for thrill-seekers and adventure lovers!

No experience required – professional instructors provide full safety training and equipment!

Half-Day ATV Program

08:00 - 08:30

Hotel Pickup

Comfortable transfer from Nha Trang to Zen Valley ATV Park

09:00 - 09:30

Safety Briefing & ATV Training

Professional instructors provide safety equipment and teach basic ATV operation

10:00 - 11:00

Mountain Trail Adventure

Ride through challenging mountain terrain with stunning valley views

11:00 - 11:30

Jungle Path & Mud Track

Navigate through dense jungle trails and muddy off-road sections

12:00 - 12:30

River Crossing & Rice Paddy Fields

Experience thrilling water crossings and ride through scenic rice paddies

13:00

Return & Hotel Drop-off

Clean up, return equipment, and transfer back to Nha Trang hotel

What's Included

  • Round-trip hotel transfers
  • 250cc ATV rental (automatic)
  • Professional English-speaking instructor
  • Safety helmet & protective gear
  • Safety briefing & training session
  • Drinking water & towels
  • Travel insurance

Not Included

  • Tips for instructors (optional)

Important Information

Driving License

Valid driving license required. Minimum age 16 years

What to Bring

Comfortable clothes, closed shoes, sunglasses, sunscreen, change of clothes

Health Notice

Not suitable for pregnant women or those with back/neck problems

Age Policy

Minimum age 16 years with license. Children can ride as passengers (age 6+)
