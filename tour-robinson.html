About This Experience

Escape to Robinson Island – an exclusive VIP island adventure inspired by the classic Robinson Crusoe tale! This unique eco-resort combines tropical paradise luxury with adventurous water sports, private beach access, and authentic Vietnamese island living.

Enjoy jet skiing, kayaking, banana boat rides, mud bathing, and relaxation in pristine nature away from crowds!
