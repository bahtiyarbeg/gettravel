About This Experience

Unleash your inner Tarzan at Kong Forest Adventure Park – Vietnam's most exciting jungle challenge course! Inspired by Kong: Skull Island, this extreme adventure features high-speed ziplines, suspended rope bridges, tree-climbing obstacles, Tarzan swings, and breathtaking jungle canopy views. Multiple difficulty levels available from beginner to expert!

Perfect for thrill-seekers, families with teenagers, and anyone craving an Indiana Jones-style adventure!
