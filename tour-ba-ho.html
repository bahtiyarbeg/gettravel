Ba Ho Waterfalls Adventure

Jungle Trek & Natural Swimming Pools

Half Day
Trekking

About This Experience

Discover Ba Ho Waterfalls ("Three Pools") – a hidden gem nestled in the pristine jungle 25km north of Nha Trang! This eco-adventure takes you through lush tropical forests to three stunning cascading waterfalls, each with crystal-clear natural swimming pools perfect for swimming, cliff jumping, and relaxation.

Perfect for nature lovers, adventure seekers, and anyone looking to escape the beach crowds!

Half-Day Program

08:00 - 08:30

Hotel Pickup

Comfortable transfer from Nha Trang (30 min scenic drive)

09:00 - 09:30

Trek to First Waterfall

Easy 15-minute jungle trail, swim in natural pool (5m depth)

10:00 - 11:00

Second Waterfall - The Main Attraction

Moderate climb (20 min), largest pool (10m deep), cliff jumping (3-5m), stunning cascade

11:30 - 12:30

Third Waterfall - Summit Pool

Challenging climb (25 min), secluded pool at mountain peak, panoramic jungle views

13:00 - 13:30

Return Journey & Drop-off

Trek back to base, transfer to Nha Trang hotel
