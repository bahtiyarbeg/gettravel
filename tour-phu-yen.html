About the Tour

🌅 A JOURNEY TO THE EDGE OF THE EARTH! Phu Yen is NOT just a beach — it's the EASTERNMOST POINT of mainland Vietnam! The place where the sun rises FIRST in the entire country! There's no mass tourism, noisy crowds or hustle here — only WILD NATURE in all its beauty, vast deserted beaches, unique basalt reefs and a truly authentic Vietnamese atmosphere!

📸 INSTAGRAM PARADISE! Dai Lanh Cape with its lighthouse on the cliff, the unique Ganh Da Dia reef (basalt columns like stone honeycombs!), deserted Bai Mon beach, Ong Kop wooden bridge, O Loan lagoon — these are the MOST beautiful photo spots in all of Vietnam!

🌊 For adventure seekers: This tour is for those who want to see the REAL Vietnam, feel freedom, open space and the power of nature. Early departure (05:00), a long but scenic coastal drive, a full day of discoveries. This is not just a tour — it's a REAL JOURNEY!

📍 What You'll See on the Tour

🏝 Dai Lanh Cape (Mũi Điện)

The easternmost point of mainland Vietnam — where the country greets its first sunrise. Panoramic ocean views, a clifftop lighthouse and an endless horizon.

🪨 Ganh Da Dia Reef (Gành Đá Đĩa)

A unique natural formation of volcanic basalt columns. The rocks look like giant stone honeycombs — one of the most photogenic places in Vietnam.

🌊 Bai Mon Beach

Crystal-clear water, soft sand and almost no tourists. A perfect spot for swimming and relaxing.

🌉 Ong Kop Wooden Bridge

A picturesque bridge over the lagoon with stunning views of mountains and fishing boats.

🏞 O Loan Lagoon

A peaceful lagoon with fresh seafood and the atmosphere of a real Vietnamese province.
