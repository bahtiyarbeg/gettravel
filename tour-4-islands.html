4 Islands Tour

Classic island hopping adventure in Nha Trang Bay

Full Day (08:00 - 17:00)
Group Tour
Speed Boat

About the Tour

🏝️ DISCOVER PARADISE! Embark on Nha Trang's MOST POPULAR island-hopping adventure! This full-day tour takes you to 4 stunning islands where turquoise waters meet white sandy beaches, creating the perfect tropical escape.

🌊 What makes this tour special: Experience the BEST of Nha Trang Bay in one incredible day! Swim in crystal-clear waters teeming with colorful fish, snorkel over vibrant coral reefs, relax on pristine beaches, and feast on fresh seafood while cruising between islands. This is the perfect introduction to Vietnam's coastal paradise!

💎 Perfect for: Families, couples, solo travelers, and groups. Whether you're seeking adventure, relaxation, or Instagram-worthy moments, this tour delivers it all!

Day Itinerary

08:00 - 08:30

Hotel Pickup

Comfortable air-conditioned transfer from your hotel to the marina

09:00 - 10:30

First Island - Snorkeling Paradise

Swimming and snorkeling in crystal-clear waters. Explore colorful coral reefs and tropical fish. Equipment provided for all guests.

11:00 - 12:00

Second Island - Fishing Village

Visit the local floating fishing village and aquarium. Learn about marine life, local culture, and traditional fishing methods.

12:30 - 14:00

Lunch on Boat

Fresh seafood buffet with Vietnamese specialties, tropical fruits, and unlimited soft drinks. Enjoy your meal while cruising between islands.

14:30 - 15:30

Third Island - Beach Relaxation

Relaxation on white sandy beach. Optional activities: kayaking, beach volleyball, swimming, or just sunbathing!

16:00 - 16:30

Fourth Island - Final Snorkeling

Last snorkeling stop at pristine coral gardens. Perfect ending to your island adventure!

17:00

Return & Drop-off

Boat returns to marina, transfer back to your hotel

⚠️ Important Information

  • Child Policy: Children under 1 meter (3.3 ft) go FREE. Children 1m-1.3m (3.3-4.3 ft) receive 50% discount. Over 1.3m charged as adults.
  • What to Bring: Swimsuit, towel, sunscreen SPF50+, sunglasses, hat, change of clothes, waterproof phone case, some cash for souvenirs.
  • Weather Policy: Tours operate in light rain. Dangerous weather conditions may result in rescheduling or full refund.
  • Health Requirements: Not recommended for pregnant women, those with back/neck problems, or serious medical conditions.
  • Swimming Ability: Basic swimming ability recommended, but life jackets provided for all guests.
  • Languages: Tour available in English, Russian, Chinese, and Korean.
