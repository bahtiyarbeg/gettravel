About the Resort

🎪 VIETNAM'S LARGEST ENTERTAINMENT COMPLEX! VinPearl (VinWonders) is not just a park — it's an entire ISLAND OF FUN! Discover 500,000 m² of pure happiness: a modern theme park, waterpark with extreme slides, a giant aquarium, zoo, cable car over the sea (3.3 km!) and pristine beaches. One day might NOT be enough!

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Perfect for families: Everything is HERE! Attractions for toddlers 3+, extreme slides for teenagers, relaxation zones for adults, romantic views for couples. Evening shows with fountains, music and fireworks will delight the whole family!

🌟 Two formats: Option 1 — 1-day ticket (packed and affordable). Option 2 — 2 days/1 night at the luxurious VinPearl hotel on the island (maximum relaxation and comfort). Choose your style of vacation!
