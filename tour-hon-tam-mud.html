Volcanic Mineral Therapy & Wellness Retreat

Half Day
Spa & Wellness
Therapy

About This Experience

Indulge in the ultimate wellness experience at Hon Tam Mud Bath & Spa! Famous for its therapeutic volcanic mineral mud, this luxury spa resort offers rejuvenating clay treatments, natural hot springs, professional massage therapies, and stunning island views. The mineral-rich mud is known for detoxifying skin, relieving stress, improving circulation, and leaving you feeling completely refreshed.

Perfect for couples, spa lovers, and anyone seeking deep relaxation and natural healing!
