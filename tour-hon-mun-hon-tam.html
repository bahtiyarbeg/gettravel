About the Tour

🌟 BEST VALUE Tour! Why visit 4 islands when 3 PERFECT ones give you MORE TIME to enjoy paradise? This smartly designed tour focuses on QUALITY over quantity, taking you to the most spectacular islands with extra time for swimming, snorkeling, and beach relaxation!

🎯 Why choose 3 Islands: LESS CROWDED, MORE RELAXATION! While others rush through 4 islands, you'll have leisurely 1.5-2 hours at each stop. More swimming time, better photo opportunities, and a more intimate island experience. Plus, you'll save money without missing anything important!

✨ Smart travelers choose this! Same stunning turquoise waters, same vibrant coral reefs, same fresh seafood lunch - just BETTER PACED and more enjoyable. Perfect for those who prefer quality time over quantity!

Day Itinerary

08:00 - 08:30

Hotel Pickup

Comfortable air-conditioned transfer from your hotel to the pier

09:00 - 11:00

First Island - Snorkeling Paradise

TWO FULL HOURS of snorkeling at pristine coral reefs. Swim with colorful tropical fish, explore underwater gardens. Equipment and life jackets provided for all guests.

11:30 - 13:30

Second Island - Beach & Lunch

Fresh seafood lunch on board while cruising. Arrive at beautiful beach for swimming, relaxation, and beach activities. Plenty of time to enjoy!

14:00 - 16:00

Third Island - Water Sports & Relaxation

Beach games, kayaking, more snorkeling opportunities, or simply relax on the beach. Your choice - no rushing!

16:30 - 17:00

Return & Drop-off

Scenic boat ride back to pier, transfer to your hotel
