About This Experience

Hon Mun Marine Protected Area is recognized as one of Southeast Asia's most beautiful underwater ecosystems! This UNESCO-acknowledged marine reserve boasts over 350 species of coral and 200+ fish species, making it a world-class snorkeling destination.

Dive into crystal-clear turquoise waters with visibility up to 20 meters, swim among vibrant coral gardens, encounter sea turtles, colorful reef fish, and explore hidden underwater caves. This eco-tourism experience combines marine conservation with unforgettable adventure!
